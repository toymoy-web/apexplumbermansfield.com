Water Line Leaking Between The Meter And The House?.

A leak on the service line running from the street meter to the house shows up as an unexplained wet spot in the yard, a running meter with everything inside off, or a sudden drop in water pressure sitewide.

Why It Happens Here.

Water Line Repair & Replacement In Mansfield, TX.

Local Cause.

North Texas' shifting clay soil puts real stress on underground water service lines over the years, and older lines are frequently galvanized steel or unlined copper, both of which corrode and eventually leak. A tree root growing toward a service line's moisture is another common cause of a slow leak that gets worse over time.

Emergency dispatch available 24/7. Standard calls get a same-day window.

Symptoms.

Signs Of A Water Line Problem.

An Unexplained Wet Spot In The Yard

Especially between the meter and the house, along the likely path of the service line.

Meter Still Running With Everything Off

Confirms a leak somewhere in the system, a water line leak is one of the most common causes.

Sitewide Low Water Pressure

A leaking or partially blocked service line reduces pressure to every fixture in the house, not just one.

A Sudden Spike In The Water Bill

Underground service line leaks can run for weeks before becoming visible above ground.

Diagnosis.

Locating The Leak.

Acoustic leak detection equipment traces the sound of water escaping under pressure along the buried line's path, which pinpoints the repair location before any digging starts, rather than trenching the whole line's length to search for it. This matters more in Mansfield than it might elsewhere, since the shifting clay soil here makes guessing at a dig location both more likely to miss and more disruptive to get wrong.

Your Options.

Repair Or Full Line Replacement.

A single, localized failure point is repaired directly. A line with multiple failure points, or one made of galvanized steel that has reached the end of its service life, is better served by a full service line replacement, trenchless where soil and access allow it. Both options are explained honestly based on what the leak location and pipe material actually show.

FAQs.

Common Questions About Water Line Repair & Replacement

How do I know if the leak is on my side or the city's side of the meter?

Anything from the meter to the house is the homeowner's responsibility, and anything before the meter is typically the city's, we can help identify which side of that line a leak is actually on before quoting anything.

Can a water line leak be fixed without digging up my whole yard?

Often yes, acoustic leak detection pinpoints the location first so any excavation is targeted rather than a full trench along the line's length.

Is a water line leak an emergency?

A significant leak affecting water pressure or causing yard flooding is treated as urgent, our line is answered 24 hours a day for exactly this kind of call.

Do you offer trenchless water line replacement in Mansfield?

Where soil condition and access allow it, yes, we assess this during the diagnosis before recommending a repair approach rather than assuming it upfront.
