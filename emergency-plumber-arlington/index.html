Arlington's Growth Decades.

A Large City With A Wide Range Of Home Ages.

Arlington had 7,692 residents in 1950 and 261,721 by 1990, and the 2020 census counted 394,266, which makes it the second-largest city in Tarrant County and the 50th largest in the country. That growth came in waves, so a single Arlington street can hold homes built decades apart.

For plumbing, home age is the first thing we ask about. Homes from the postwar boom and the decades after it are the likeliest to still have original galvanized supply lines, aging drain lines, or a water heater that has already been replaced more than once. Newer homes are more likely to call about hard-water scale, builder-grade fixtures, or a tankless unit that needs descaling.

Local Signals.

Why Arlington Homes Call Us.

Home Age Varies Block To Block.

Because Arlington grew from under 8,000 people in 1950 to more than 260,000 by 1990, the most useful first question on any call is when the house was built.

Hard North Texas Water.

Municipal water across North Texas generally runs hard, and Arlington homes see the same scale in water heaters, aerators, and shower fixtures that Mansfield homes do.

Slab Foundations On Expansive Clay.

Most homes in this part of Tarrant County sit on concrete slabs over expansive clay, so a leak under the slab shows up as a warm spot, damp carpet, or a water bill that climbs for no clear reason.

Two Cities, One Market.

Mansfield ISD runs campuses inside Arlington, including Kenneth Davis and Imogene Gideon elementaries, so many south Arlington families already think of Mansfield as their home market.

Before We Arrive.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak.

1. Shut Off The Main Valve.

Stop the water at the source, this limits damage more than anything else you can do while help is on the way.

2. Kill Power To Wet Areas.

If it is safe to reach the breaker without stepping into water, cut power to the affected area.

3. Clear The Immediate Area.

Move furniture, rugs, and valuables away from the water if it is safe to do so.

4. Call Our 24/7 Line.

A real person answers, day or night, and a technician is dispatched to your Arlington address right away.
