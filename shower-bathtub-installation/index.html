Diagnosis.

What We Check Before Installing.

Before setting a new tub or shower, we confirm the drain location and slope. We also check the condition of the supply lines and valve behind the wall, and verify the subfloor has no existing water damage that needs addressing first, so the new installation is not covering up a problem rather than fixing it. This step matters most on an older Mansfield home where the original plumbing behind the wall has never been opened up or inspected since the house was built.

Your Options.

Installation.

Installation includes setting the new fixture level and properly supported, connecting and pressure-testing the water lines, installing or replacing the drain assembly, and confirming a proper watertight seal at every connection before any finish work covers it up.

FAQs.

Common Questions About Shower & Bathtub Installation

Can you convert my bathtub to a walk-in shower in Mansfield?

Yes, tub-to-shower conversions are a common request, we handle the drain, valve, and plumbing side of the conversion.

Do you coordinate with a contractor for a full bathroom remodel?

Yes, we regularly work alongside contractors and tile installers, handling the plumbing scope of the project.

Will you check for water damage before installing a new tub or shower?

Yes, checking the subfloor and existing valve condition before installation is part of the process, covering up an existing problem is not the goal.

Can you replace just the shower valve without replacing the whole shower?

Yes, a standalone valve replacement is possible when the fixture itself is in good condition, we assess which makes sense during the visit.

How long does a tub-to-shower conversion take?

A straightforward conversion is often completed in a day or two, a full bathroom remodel involving new tile and fixtures takes longer, we walk through the timeline before starting.

Do you handle the tile and finish work too, or just the plumbing?

We handle the plumbing scope, the drain, valve, and water line connections, and coordinate with your tile installer or contractor on the finish work.
