Smell Gas, Or Need A New Gas Line Run?.

A gas smell near an appliance, a gas line for a new range or outdoor grill, or a failed gas line inspection during a home sale are the situations that actually bring people to this page. All three require a licensed plumber, not a DIY fix.

Why It Happens Here.

Gas Line Installation & Repair In Mansfield, TX.

Local Cause.

Older Mansfield homes sometimes still have galvanized or undersized gas lines from an earlier construction era. Those lines were never sized for a modern range, tankless water heater, or outdoor kitchen added later, which is the most common reason a new gas line run gets requested rather than a repair.

Diagnosis.

How We Handle It.

Any suspected leak is pressure-tested to confirm and locate it before anything is opened up. A new line run is sized correctly for the appliance's actual BTU demand, not just matched to whatever pipe was already there, and every job is permitted and inspected per the jurisdiction the home sits in.

Safety.

If You Smell Gas.

  • Leave the house immediately, do not operate any light switch, appliance, or phone inside.
  • Do not attempt to locate the leak yourself or light anything, including a pilot light.
  • Call your gas utility's emergency line from outside the home, then call us.
  • Do not return inside until the utility or a licensed technician confirms it is safe.
Your Options.

Installation And Repair.

A confirmed leak is repaired and pressure-tested again before the line is put back into service. A new line run is installed, pressure-tested, and permitted correctly the first time, since a failed inspection later costs more time than doing it right at the start.

FAQs.

Common Questions About Gas Line Installation & Repair

Is a suspected gas leak always an emergency in Mansfield?

Yes, leave the area, do not operate switches or appliances, and call your gas utility's emergency line from outside before calling us.

Do you handle gas line work for a new outdoor kitchen or grill?

Yes, we run and permit new gas lines for outdoor kitchens, grills, and fire features.

Can you fix what a home inspection flagged on my gas line?

Yes, we regularly correct gas line issues found during pre-sale home inspections in Mansfield.

Do I need a permit for a new gas line in Mansfield?

Yes, gas line work requires a permit and inspection in this jurisdiction, we handle that as part of the job.

Can you upsize an existing gas line for a new tankless water heater?

Yes, tankless units often demand more gas flow than the old line was sized for, we calculate the correct pipe size for the new load before running it.
