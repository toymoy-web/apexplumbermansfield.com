The Call We Get Most From Burleson.

A Warm Spot On The Floor Or A Bill That Jumped.

The most common Burleson call starts the same way it does across this whole coverage area: a warm spot on the floor, a water bill that jumped with no clear reason, or a toilet that will not stop running. Burleson's housing stock ranges from an original town core built when the Missouri-Kansas-Texas Railroad, known locally as "the Katy," first put Burleson on the map in the 1880s, out through newer subdivisions built during the city's rapid recent growth. The population grew from under 37,000 in 2010 to well over 57,000 today.

That growth pattern matters for diagnosis. An older home closer to Burleson's original center is more likely to be dealing with aging galvanized supply lines or a sewer lateral old enough to have real root intrusion. A newer home in one of Burleson's growth-era subdivisions is more likely calling about a builder-grade fixture or a tankless water heater already showing hard-water scale. We ask about the home's approximate age on the first call for exactly this reason.

Before We Arrive.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak.

1. Shut Off The Main Valve.

Stop the water at the source, this limits damage more than anything else you can do while help is on the way.

2. Kill Power To Wet Areas.

If it is safe to reach the breaker without stepping into water, cut power to the affected area.

3. Clear The Immediate Area.

Move furniture, rugs, and valuables away from the water if it is safe to do so.

4. Call Our 24/7 Line.

A real person answers, day or night, and a technician is dispatched to your Burleson address right away.
