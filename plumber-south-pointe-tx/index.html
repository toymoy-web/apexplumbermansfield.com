Neighborhood Snapshot.

What Defines South Pointe.

Where It Is.

South Pointe lies near the U.S. 287 and State Highway 360 corridor and is part of the Mansfield Independent School District.

Size.

It was announced in 2016 as an 870-acre master-planned community, with plans for four future school sites inside it.

Lot Sizes.

Builders offered homes on 50-foot, 65-foot, and 75-foot homesites. David Weekley Homes called these its Cottage, Village, and Manor series.

Amenities.

Announced features include an amenity center, a resort-style pool, ponds, trails, and parks.

Housing In South Pointe.

A Community Built Since 2016.

South Pointe is among the newer places to live in Mansfield. David Weekley Homes announced its first sales in February 2016, and other builders, including Grand Homes and Coventry Homes, have offered homes here as well. Building has continued in phases, so a street in one part of South Pointe may be a few years older than a street a short distance away.

New homes are not free of plumbing needs. In the first years we expect calls about fixtures that need adjusting, water heaters that need setup changes, and the effect of local water on new appliances. We also expect calls from owners who want upgrades the builder did not include, such as water filtration or a softener.

Because many homes in South Pointe are still fairly new, the most useful thing a plumber can offer is often prevention. A short visit to check shutoff valves, water pressure, and the water heater can catch small problems before they turn into damage.

Newer Home Priorities.

What New Homeowners Often Ask About.

Know Your Shutoffs.

Every household member should know where the main water shutoff is and how to turn it. In a burst line or failed supply hose, those seconds decide how much damage there is.

Water Pressure.

Pressure that is too high stresses fixtures, hoses, and appliance valves. A gauge reading and, if needed, a pressure regulator adjustment is a quick check.

Filtration And Softening.

Many owners of new homes add a softener or filter after seeing spotting and scale. We install both and can explain which fits your water and household.

Tankless Heaters.

Newer homes are more likely to have tankless units, which need periodic descaling in hard water to keep their heat exchangers working properly.

FAQs.

Common Questions From South Pointe.

Is South Pointe part of Mansfield?

South Pointe is marketed as a Mansfield community and is served by Mansfield ISD. If you need to know whether your address is inside city limits for permits or utilities, the City of Mansfield can confirm.

Do you work on brand new homes?

Yes. New homes still need service, whether that is a fixture that leaks, a water heater issue, or an upgrade like a softener or filter. Check your builder warranty first for defects that may be covered.

Can you install a water softener in a South Pointe home?

Yes. We install and repair softeners, and we can talk through whether a softener, a filter, or both fit your water and household.

How do I stop a leak until a plumber arrives?

Shut off the water at the main valve or at the fixture's own shutoff, turn off power to wet areas if it is safe to reach the breaker, and call us. We will tell you what to do next.
