Diagnosis.

Assessing The Damage.

Moisture meters and thermal imaging identify how far water has actually traveled into walls, flooring, and framing, not just what is visible on the surface. This assessment determines what can be dried in place and what needs to be removed and replaced, and it is the basis for the documentation used in an insurance claim, not a guess made from a visual walkthrough alone.

Safety.

What To Do Before Help Arrives.

  • If it is safe to do so, shut off electricity to any area with standing water before entering.
  • Do not use household electronics or outlets in or near standing water.
  • Move valuables and furniture out of the affected area if you can do so safely.
  • Photograph the damage for insurance purposes before cleanup begins if there is time to do so safely.
Your Options.

The Restoration Process.

Water extraction comes first, followed by structural drying with commercial dehumidifiers and air movers, then assessment of what materials can be salvaged versus what needs replacement. Where the underlying plumbing issue has not yet been repaired, that gets addressed first, restoration on top of an active leak does not hold.

FAQs.

Common Questions About Water Damage Restoration

Is water damage restoration the same as fixing the plumbing problem that caused it?

No, these are two separate services, the plumbing repair stops the water, restoration is the cleanup, drying, and structural recovery afterward.

How quickly should water damage be addressed in Mansfield?

As soon as possible, water soaking into drywall and flooring causes more damage the longer it sits, and mold risk increases significantly after about 24 to 48 hours.

Will insurance cover water damage restoration?

Coverage depends on your specific policy and the cause of the damage, we provide documentation of the damage and the work performed to support your claim.

Do you handle both the water extraction and the structural drying?

Yes, extraction, structural drying, and damage assessment are all part of the restoration process we handle.

What happens if mold has already started growing?

Extended moisture exposure can lead to mold growth, and that changes the scope of the work, we assess for it during the initial visit and address it as part of the restoration plan.
