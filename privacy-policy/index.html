Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled in connection with apexplumbermansfield.com.

Information.

Information We Collect.

Mansfield Apex Plumbing is a call-only advertising and referral service. We do not collect personal information through website forms, since this site intentionally has none. Information is collected when a visitor calls the phone number listed on this site, which is routed to a licensed, participating plumbing contractor for scheduling and service.

Standard, non-personally-identifying information may be collected automatically through normal web server logs and analytics, such as browser type, approximate location, and pages visited, used only to understand site usage and improve the experience for future visitors.

Use.

How Information Is Used.

Information shared during a phone call, such as name, address, and the nature of the plumbing issue, is used solely to schedule and provide the requested service. It is not sold to third parties.

Call recordings, where used for quality assurance or training purposes, are handled in accordance with applicable Texas law regarding call recording consent.

Third Parties.

Third-Party Services.

This site may use third-party analytics or advertising tracking tools to measure site performance. These tools do not have access to any information shared during a phone call.

Contact.

Questions About This Policy.

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to contact@apexplumbermansfield.com or by calling (817) 601-6266.
