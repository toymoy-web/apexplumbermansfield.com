Downtown Venus And The New Growth Around It.

A Historic Core Surrounded By New Construction.

Venus's historic downtown sits around Venus Square, at West Second Street and South Walnut Street. By the 1920s the town already had its own schools, a small college, several businesses, and a weekly newspaper, the Venus Express. That older core sees the plumbing issues you would expect from a home several decades old, aging supply lines and fixtures original to the property.

Around that historic center, Venus has grown fast, from 910 residents in 2000 to 2,960 in 2010 and 4,361 in 2020, as the Metroplex's growth pushed south. That newer housing calls about different things, tankless water heater maintenance and builder-grade fixture issues rather than the aging pipe concerns of the historic downtown area.

Before We Arrive.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak.

1. Shut Off The Main Valve.

Stop the water at the source, this limits damage more than anything else you can do while help is on the way.

2. Kill Power To Wet Areas.

If it is safe to reach the breaker without stepping into water, cut power to the affected area.

3. Clear The Immediate Area.

Move furniture, rugs, and valuables away from the water if it is safe to do so.

4. Call Our 24/7 Line.

A real person answers, day or night, and a technician is dispatched to your Venus address right away.
