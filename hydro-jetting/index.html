Hydro Jetting in Mansfield, TX.

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the full inside diameter of a drain or sewer line clean, rather than just breaking a path through a clog the way a standard drain snake does. It is the difference between clearing a hole through a blockage and actually removing what caused it, which is why a line that keeps clogging on a regular schedule is usually a jetting candidate, not another round of snaking.

Still Backing Up After Snaking?.

If a drain that was snaked recently is already slow or backing up again, the underlying cause, usually grease buildup or root intrusion, was not actually removed, only punched through. That is the situation hydro jetting is built for.

Why It Happens Here.

Hydro Jetting In Mansfield, TX.

Local Cause.

In Mansfield's clay soil, older sewer laterals are prone to root intrusion at pipe joints, and grease buildup in kitchen lines narrows pipe diameter over years regardless of a home's age. Both build up gradually along the entire pipe wall, which is exactly why a spot-treatment like snaking keeps needing to be repeated on the same line.

Diagnosis.

How A Jetting Visit Works.

We typically run a camera through the line first. That confirms the cause and checks for any structural pipe damage jetting alone will not fix, then we jet the line with pressure calibrated to the pipe material and condition. Too much pressure in an already compromised older pipe can cause more harm than good, which is why the camera check comes first.

Your Options.

Jetting Versus Snaking Versus Repair.

Snaking clears an immediate blockage; jetting clears the whole pipe of built-up grease, scale, and root intrusion. If the camera inspection shows the pipe itself is cracked, offset, or collapsed rather than just fouled, jetting will not fix that, see our sewer line repair page for what comes next. For a single, isolated slow drain, standard drain cleaning is usually enough on its own.

FAQs.

Common Questions About Hydro Jetting

How long does a hydro jetting visit take?

A typical residential line takes under an hour once the camera inspection confirms jetting is the right approach, a longer commercial kitchen line can take more time.

Is hydro jetting safe for older Mansfield homes' pipes?

We calibrate pressure to the pipe's material and condition, and run a camera inspection first on older lines to check for existing damage before jetting.

How often does a line need to be jetted?

It depends heavily on use, a heavy-grease kitchen line or a root-prone sewer lateral may need it every year or two, most residential lines need it far less often.

Do you offer hydro jetting for commercial kitchens in Mansfield?

Yes, commercial kitchen grease-line jetting is one of the more common commercial calls we get.

Can jetting damage my pipes?

Properly calibrated jetting is safe for sound pipe, which is why we check the line's condition with a camera first rather than jetting blind.
