Water Meter Running With Everything Turned Off?.

A water meter that keeps spinning after every faucet in the house is off, or a water bill that jumped with no obvious cause, is one of the most common reasons Mansfield homeowners call us. Most of the time it means a leak somewhere you cannot see, not a meter problem.

Why It Happens Here.

Leak Detection In Mansfield, TX.

Local Cause.

Mansfield's slab-on-grade construction means a leaking hot or cold water line often runs under the concrete itself, invisible until it shows up as a warm spot on the floor or unexplained dampness. Older copper plumbing, common in original-era Mansfield homes, is also prone to pinhole leaks at soldered joints as it ages, and irrigation lines can leak underground for months before a yard shows any visible sign.

Diagnosis.

How We Find It Without Guessing.

Electronic leak detection equipment listens for the acoustic signature of water escaping under pressure, and thermal imaging can spot temperature differences a hot water line leak creates under a slab. Combined with isolating sections of the plumbing system one at a time, this pinpoints the leak's actual location before any concrete or wall is opened, rather than guessing and cutting exploratory holes. The same equipment works for an underground leak between the meter and the house, a common source of an unexplained high water bill.

Your Options.

What Happens After The Leak Is Found.

Once located, the fix depends on where the leak is. A slab leak gets scoped for slab leak detection and repair. A leak in an accessible wall or under-sink line is usually a straightforward repair on its own, and a failing section of old copper may point toward a broader repiping conversation if it is not an isolated issue. Either way, the goal of detection is the same, know exactly what is wrong before anything gets opened up.

FAQs.

Common Questions About Leak Detection

How can I tell if I have a leak before calling in Mansfield?

Turn off every faucet and water-using appliance, then check if the water meter's leak indicator or dial is still moving, if it is, there is a leak somewhere in the system.

Can you find a leak without cutting into my walls or slab?

In most cases yes, acoustic and thermal leak detection equipment pinpoints the location first, so any opening made is targeted rather than exploratory.

Is a running water meter always a leak?

Almost always, a small amount of movement can be a running toilet or an ice maker filling, but continuous movement with everything off points to a real leak.

Do you offer commercial leak detection in Mansfield?

Yes, we handle commercial leak detection for businesses and property managers, including grease trap and floor drain related leaks.
